The budget was 130,000 dollars and the site had no services. That decided almost everything: a wood stove instead of a mechanical system, a slab on grade, and a frame simple enough that a local contractor could put it up in three weeks.

The two doors sit on adjacent faces rather than opposite ones. It sounds like a small thing. It is the difference between a room that stays warm when the door opens and one that does not.

It has been open two winters. The city reports it is used most heavily at dusk on weekdays, which was not what any of us predicted, and the benches have been moved from where we drew them. Both are fine. The stove is the thing people came for.
