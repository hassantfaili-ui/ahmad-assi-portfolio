The site consists of two distinct parcels: 13904 Hayne Boulevard, approximately 7,000 square feet, and 13900 Hayne Boulevard, approximately 6,000 square feet, under separate ownership. At present only the Lincoln Beach Center occupies the space, surrounded by undeveloped grassland and lacking both public outdoor amenities and site programming.

Phase one introduces a significant transformation, expanding the museum and adding a central outdoor courtyard with seating and a water fountain. A three storey restaurant structure is also added, the first two levels offering dining and the third functioning as an elevated lookout over the levee. In an emergency, all elevated spaces double as a platform during floods.

Phase two involves acquiring the adjacent parcel to the west, made possible through funds generated by the restaurant, museum activities and community support. This expansion introduces a community garden, informal seating and improved circulation across both parcels, strengthening the site's social and ecological connectivity.
