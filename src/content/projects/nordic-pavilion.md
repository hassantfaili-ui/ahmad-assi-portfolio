For two months there is no daylight in Tromso. Most entries treated that as a lighting problem. It is a planning problem: if the building cannot be organised around the sun, it has to be organised around something else, and the obvious candidate is the fjord.

The plan spirals. A visitor moves outward through the galleries, and each turn presents a different length of water, so the orientation comes from the view rather than from the compass. The lit interior becomes the landmark in the dark half of the year, which is a straightforward thing for a building on a waterfront to do and an easy one to get sentimental about.

Shortlisted, and then not selected. The winning entry did something braver with the roof than I did.
