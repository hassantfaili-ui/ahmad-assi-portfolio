The 1974 branch worked well enough inside and was almost impossible to enter. Its door faced a parking lot at the back of a berm, and the park side, where everyone actually arrives from, presented a blank wall.

The addition is a single move: a low bar along the park edge with the new entrance at the point where the two desire lines already cross. Inside, it holds the reading room, a programme space that can be locked off after hours, and the staff areas the old building never had.

The reading room is the part I care about. It faces southwest with a clerestory above the stacks, and the floor is heated, because a library in this city is a public living room for five months of the year and should be detailed like one.
