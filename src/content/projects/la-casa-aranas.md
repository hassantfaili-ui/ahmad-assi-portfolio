A full-scale elevated house, currently under construction, with a split-bedroom layout, open-concept living areas and integrated outdoor terraces. The design emphasizes efficient spatial organization and structural balance.

Raising the house creates a sheltered ground level that stays usable through heavy rain, and puts the living areas above the wet season.
