Aggregate pits north of the city are abandoned once the gravel is gone, and they fill with groundwater. The thesis took one of them as a site that had already been excavated, at enormous expense, by someone with no interest in architecture.

Three halls step down the slope at three temperatures, and the water moves between them by gravity. The coldest is open to the sky and to the pit walls, which are raw. The warmest is the deepest, lit from a single opening, and it is the room the whole project exists to arrive at.

It is a student project and it shows in places, particularly in how lightly it treats the structure of the pit wall. I keep it in the set because the question it asks, what a warm public room is worth in a cold country, is the one I am still working on.
